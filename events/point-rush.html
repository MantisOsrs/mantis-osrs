Point Rush

Rules
  • Point Rush lasts two weeks. The team with the most points at the end wins.
  • Drops must be submitted to the "event-drops" discord channel with the event tag in screenshot.
  • Each team member may use a maximum of two accounts. Multi-logging is not permitted.
  • All teams begin in Tier I and earn points to unlock higher tiers.
  • Tiers unlock at 10, 25, 40, 60, 90, 120, and 150 base drop points.
  • Only unlocked-tier items can be submitted. Previously unlocked tiers remain available.
  • Each item card shows what its next drop is worth.
  • Every duplicate will decrease the value of that item by 1 point.
  • Tier I items can decrease to 0 points. Items in every other tier stop decreasing at 1 point.
  • Items grouped under one card are tracked and scored separately.
  • Collection Log-only items do not award points if the account obtained them before the event.
  • Unlocking each new tier grants one wheel spin.
  • Each tier contains one hidden power-up and one hidden blockage. Each can trigger only once and is revealed when activated.
  • When a power-up requires an opponent choice, no drops can be recorded until the choice is confirmed.
  • A blockage restricts what the affected team can obtain until its requirement is cleared.
  • Power-ups, blockages, and wheel spins can change standings points but do not advance or reduce tier progression.

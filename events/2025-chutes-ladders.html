Chutes & Ladders

Created by Pyros

Rules
  1. The Dice is numbered 1–3. Each team will have two re-rolls. Once the re-rolls are used, a team must complete whatever tile they land on to proceed.
  2. Each Completion must have snapshot proof of item, pet, etc. They then will need to be posted in the Discord channel for the event.
  3. Every submission must have the clan’s event tag present somewhere. (We recommend you use the Clan Events plugin on RuneLite.)
  4. A red box indicates a slide. If you land on this tile you will have two choices. First, you can take the slide; second, you can complete the task. A team can opt for the slide at any time. Each team can hit the same slide twice before they are immune.
  5. Ladder tiles are conditional. If you land on a ladder tile, you will go for the normal task. While attempting to complete said task, if your team obtains the pet from it, you can skip the rest of the task and use the ladder.
  6. Building tasks can be completed by a team, as long as all necessary pieces have been obtained.
  7. All players are limited to one account. Once you pick your account, drops on another do not count.
  8. In order to complete the race, you must complete the last tile. Good luck.

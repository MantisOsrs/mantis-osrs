Tile Race

Created by TheDog & Tactical Jokr

Rules
  1. You will spin a wheel to move 1 or 2 spaces located in the #random-wheel channel.
  2. You MUST do all red tiles.
  3. You may do either the yellow or green path. Both may be completed simultaneously.
  4. Roll from the yellow tile or the final green tile.
  5. You can land on the orange “Go Back” tile three times. On your fourth time landing there, move forward one tile.
  6. You can land on the purple “Go Back” tile three times. On your fourth time landing there, move forward one tile.
  7. Only submit fully completed tiles to the #event-drops channel. Keep multiple screenshots in team chat until complete.

Good luck and have fun!

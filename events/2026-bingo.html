Bingo

Created by TheDog & Pond Scum

Rules
  1. The event will run for two weeks.
  2. Teams will compete using traditional Bingo rules by completing tiles and forming Bingos on the event board.
  3. A team that completes the entire board within the two-week event period wins immediately.
  4. If no team completes the full board, the team with the most completed Bingos at the end of the event wins.
  5. If teams are tied in completed Bingos, the winner will be determined by the total number of individual tiles completed.

Select a player to view every drop they received throughout the event.
